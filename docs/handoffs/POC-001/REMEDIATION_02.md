## 3. Identity, replay and “fence” accuracy boundaries

- **What a mock can prove:** within the defined single-process/synthetic auth/ledger lifetime and with explicitly modeled mock ETags, a guarded same-op request may avoid duplicate upstream dispatch and a strong CAS may reject a stale writer. Mock tests can demonstrate controlled behavior for alternate legal WebDAV representations.
- **What it cannot prove:** exactly-once across processes, across restarts, after expiry/eviction without durable identity, on a provider that changes its ETags unexpectedly, or for multi-object backup/restore. Even HMAC on a record does not provide a global operation ledger or atomic server-side commit.
- **Fence acceptance contract:** `not_committed` may be terminal **only** if a documented, verifiable protocol prevents a delayed original request from later winning; a non-changing ETag, missing ETag, unexpected provider response, stale same-content hash, unknown MKCOL semantics or a timeout is **not such proof**. Preserve `UNKNOWN` and separately report possible liveness limitation rather than fabricate success/failure. No automatic fence after a user's timeout unless already approved as an explicit safe action in the original POC; it may affect concurrent writer revisions.
- **Replay acceptance contract:** a lost ledger record is not evidence a prior mutation was never attempted. If the runtime has no authoritative record of an old operation ID, do not claim `Operation-Replayed`, `SAVED` or `not_saved` solely from a new request. Document explicit bounded protection window and restart handling; if seamless replay recovery is needed, a **separate durable ledger/storage contract** and independent provider evidence are required.
- **PROPFIND and RFCs:** WebDAV [RFC 4918](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4918.html) identifies XML DAV namespace by URI, not a textual prefix; HTTP [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html) permits a comma inside a single quoted opaque ETag even though `If-Match` is also a list field. Security-oriented subset rejection is permissible as a documented limitation of this POC, **not a claim that a compliant provider is malformed**.
- **Untrusted restore:** content hashes do not authenticate an exported archive. No external untrusted JSON may assert somebody else's confirmed history. Rejected/partial import must not mutate the active synthetic namespace; safe restore must still be independently tested under the POC-07 bounded contract.
